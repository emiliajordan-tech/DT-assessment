Celebrating Our Success

The Wellington East Girls' College 1st XI Football Team takes pride in the dedication, determination, and teamwork that have shaped our journey over the years. This page celebrates the milestones and successes our team has achieved through hard work, commitment, and a passion for the game. Each accomplishment reflects the effort of every player, coach, and supporter who has contributed to the team's continued growth.

DYNASTY Premier Senior Girls Division

For over five consecutive years, the Wellington East Girls' College Football 1st XI has proudly qualified from among the top secondary school teams in the Wellington region to compete in the DYNASTY Premier Senior Girls Football Competition, the highest level of secondary school competition in the region.

WINNERS of the Premier Senior Girls Division League 2026

Wellington East Girls' College Football 1st XI put up a mighty battle against Wellington Girls College on the 12th of August in the final of the highest level of secondary school competition in the region, and came out with a win of 2-0!
