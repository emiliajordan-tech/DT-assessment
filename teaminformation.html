About the Team

The Wellington East Girls' College 1st XI Football Team is more than just a football team – we're a close-knit group of players who support, encourage, and challenge one another to be our best. Every season, our squad is made up of dedicated students from across the school, united by a shared love of the game and a commitment to representing WEGC with pride. Whether it's training, match day, or cheering each other on from the sidelines, teamwork is at the heart of everything we do. Get to know some of the players who make up our team and discover what makes the WEGC 1st XI such a special group to be part of.
