About the website

Welcome to the Wellington East Girls College 1st XI Football Team Website! This website has been created to keep players, parents, and caregivers informed and up to date on team news, achievements, fixtures, and important updates throughout the season. It also gives incoming students and their families an opportunity to learn more about the team, our values, and what it means to be a part of the WEGC football programme.

Latest News

Wellington East Girls College 1st XI Football Team has just qualified for the CAPITAL FOOTBALL TROPHY FINALS! A very strong performance was put up last night against Newlands College, leading to a winning victory of 4-0. That win qualified the team for the finals next Wednesday 12th at 6:30pm Wakefield Park against Wellington Girls' College. Come down to support if you can because it will be a great game!

What's in the near future?

The Wellington East Girls College 1st XI Football Team has qualified to head to Tāupo for the NZSS (New Zealand Secondary Schools) national football tournament! It is a 5 day tournament occuring from the 30th August - 4th September. Last year, the team placed the highest out of all the Wellington regional schools, and this year they plan to achieve that and more! Make sure to keep updated as it will be a tournament to remember!
